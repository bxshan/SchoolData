[ORG-1] recognizes achievement for art direction in film. The category's original name was [GPE-1], but was changed to its current name in 2012 for [ORG-2]. This change resulted from [ORG-3] branch of [ORG-4] (AMPAS) being renamed the [ORG-5]' branch. Since 1947, the award is shared with the set decorators. It is awarded to the best interior design in a film.

The films below are listed with their production year (for example, the 2000 [ORG-6] is given to a film from 1999). In the lists below, the winner of the award for each year is shown first, followed by the other nominees in alphabetical order.
